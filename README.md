# StudioOps

**Production intelligence for film & television.**

Describe your project in plain language, and StudioOps hands back a structured
Studio Brief — comparable titles, market signals, audience insight, the
competitive landscape, opportunities, and risks — with every claim traced to a
real source you can open and read.

---

## The problem

Every development and greenlight decision rides on information that is scattered
and moving fast:

- What did comparable titles actually do at market?
- Who is commissioning in this territory right now?
- Where are audiences heading?
- What incentives, partners, or distribution routes apply?
- What could quietly sink the production?

Piecing this together means hours across trade press, databases, and half-remembered
deals — or deciding on instinct and hoping. And the obvious shortcut, asking a
generic AI chatbot, is worse than no answer: it invents box-office figures, makes
up comparable films, and cites sources that don't exist. For a decision worth
millions, that's not intelligence. It's a liability.

## The solution

StudioOps is a research assistant built for producers, development execs, and
financiers. You give it a brief; it does the legwork a studio analyst would spend
a day on, and returns it in under a minute — grounded, structured, and cited.

The difference that matters: **StudioOps never makes things up.** Every figure,
comparable, and claim links back to a real page it found on the live web. Anything
the research couldn't confirm is listed plainly as an open question, not quietly
filled in with a guess. You can trust the brief because you can check it.

## How it works

You describe the project — a sentence or two is enough, and you can add details
like format, genre, and territory if you want to sharpen the research. Then
StudioOps works through four steps, and you watch each one happen live:

1. **Understands your brief.** It reads what you're making, who it's for, and
   what you need to know.
2. **Searches the live web.** It runs a set of targeted searches — comparables,
   market, audience, competition, production, distribution — and you see each one
   return in real time.
3. **Analyzes the findings.** It reads what it found and pulls out the signals,
   patterns, opportunities, and risks that matter for your decision.
4. **Builds your Studio Brief.** It assembles everything into a clean, structured
   document you can read on screen, download, and forward.

## What you get

A Studio Brief laid out for a fast, confident read:

- **Executive summary** — the headline takeaway in a few lines.
- **Comparable projects** — titles like yours and how they performed.
- **Market landscape** — what's happening in the market, with the figures the
  sources actually reported.
- **Audience intelligence** — who the audience is and where they're moving.
- **Competitive landscape** — who else is in this space and where the gaps are.
- **Production opportunities** — routes, partners, and angles worth pursuing.
- **Risks & considerations** — what to watch, rated by severity, with suggested
  actions.
- **Recommended next steps** — concrete moves to take from here.
- **What we could not confirm** — the honest list of open questions the research
  didn't answer.
- **Sources** — every page behind the brief, one click away.

Download it as a document to forward to a financier or partner, or keep the link
to come back to it.

## Try it

Open StudioOps, pick one of the example projects — like *Nigerian crime thriller*
or *Afrobeats documentary* — or write your own, and press **Start Research**.
Watch the searches run, and read your brief when it lands. Typically 20–60
seconds from idea to evidenced brief.
